Why Indian Coffee

An origin still rarely seen on American menus.

India's specialty sector is growing quickly, and its best lots deserve a place in American roasteries. We bring those coffees to the United States and stand behind where each one came from.

Shade-grown, often intercropped with pepper and cardamom, and processed with real care — Indian coffee offers profiles your customers have probably never tasted from a bag on your shelf.
