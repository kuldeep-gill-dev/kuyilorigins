Working Together

How we work with roasters

Educational Cuppings

Cuppings that introduce the coffees, estates, and processing styles behind each lot. We can walk you and your team through what makes each coffee distinct, from how it was fermented and dried to what to look for in the cup, so you can see how it might sit on your menu.

Whether it’s a tasting for your team or an evening for your customers, tell us what would be useful and we’ll plan it with you.

Roaster Collaboration

We work with roasters to find coffees that fit their menu, roasting style, and customers, and we’re glad to talk through roast approaches and how a coffee might sit alongside what you already offer.

We’d also love to feature your roasting on our website. For any coffee you buy from us that we’re not selling roasted under our own label, we can link to your website, so customers who discover that coffee through Kuyil can still find it and buy it roasted by you. It puts the producer’s story in front of more people, and sends them your way.

Full Lot Detail

Estate, variety, process, harvest, altitude, and region shared clearly for every lot.

Where the producer has them, we also share the finer details, such as fermentation, drying, and a suggested roast direction, so you can judge a coffee on more than its name.

Estate Stories

Photos, background, and producer stories you can use to help tell the story of the coffee.

Each estate has its own page introducing the people behind the coffee, and we’re happy to share more on request.
