Our Producers

The people behind the coffee.

Our current coffees come from four estates in Karnataka. Each one is chosen through estate visits and direct conversations with the people who run it.

Ratnagiri Estate

Karnataka, India

Experimental by instinct, with processing decisions made lot by lot and the people behind those trials driving what the coffee becomes.

Mysore Plantations

Karnataka, India

Women-owned, and treating Robusta as a specialty coffee in its own right rather than a commodity to be blended away.

Harley Estate

Karnataka, India

Women-led, with conservation shaping how the land is managed and a clear estate identity behind the coffee we selected.

Balanoor Plantations

Karnataka, India

A long estate history in Karnataka, with sustainability practices that shape the coffees it produces today.

Full producer profiles — their people, landscapes, varieties and practices — are being written with each estate.
