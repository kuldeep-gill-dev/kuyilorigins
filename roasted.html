Coffees shaped by four very different estates

Our current selection comes from four estates in Karnataka, each with its own approach to farming, processing, and flavor.

Ratnagiri Estate

Karnataka, India

Experimental by instinct, with processing decisions made lot by lot and the people behind those trials driving what the coffee becomes.

Mysore Plantations

Karnataka, India

Women-owned, and treating Robusta as a specialty coffee in its own right rather than a commodity to be blended away.

Harley Estate

Karnataka, India

Women-led, with conservation shaping how the land is managed and a clear estate identity behind the coffee we selected.

What Stays With Every Coffee

The estate name. The process. The people behind it.

We want you to know more than the country on the label. Each coffee stays connected to the estate it came from and the decisions that shaped it.

Our Approach

Coffee with a sense of place

We spend time with the estates we source from and choose lots with their own character. That means every coffee can be traced back to the people, landscape, and process behind it, rather than stopping at a country name.

Every coffee begins with a producer we know and a place we’ve spent time in.
