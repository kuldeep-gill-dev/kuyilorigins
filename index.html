Our Story

From Indian estates, by way of Atlanta.

Kuyil Origins was founded by Sukhpreet Gill, who selects every lot in person. Each coffee comes out of time spent on the estate: walking the blocks, cupping with the people who grew it, and understanding the decisions behind the crop.

Atlanta is where that work meets a community — the roasters, buyers and drinkers we share these coffees with.
